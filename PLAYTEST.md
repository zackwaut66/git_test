## Intended full progression loop

1. Read the title-screen premise and begin the March.
2. Follow the active Directive and clear Forsaken Farmstead.
3. Secure loot, equip recovered gear and improve the Hunter Hall.
4. Reclaim Enclave land and choose a permanent district specialization instead of receiving a fixed build.
5. Clear Hollow Causeway, use salvage/crafting and continue developing Hunters and the settlement.
6. Push through Saint Orra Chapel and defeat the Penitent Warden.
7. Use the post-Warden Hunt Board and Last Toll mastery hunt for repeatable PvE progression.
8. Continue reclaiming the six Enclave districts and upgrade Bastion, Arsenal, Foundry or Vigil Shrine structures through their tiers.
9. Form the Guild, build contribution and use coordinated Guild War preparation/attacks.
10. Enter the Realm layer, train the persistent War Host, compare rival powers and regional standings, and schedule a Realm War.
11. Assign Vanguard, Duelist and Physician strike teams to objectives during Preparation.
12. Execute the limited coordinated attacks during War, claim Results rewards and improve Prestige, Influence and the persistent realm record.
13. Repeat expeditions, contracts, kingdom construction and wars so PvE resources feed kingdom strength and kingdom/guild bonuses feed subsequent combat and wars.

## Fixed design pillars to verify

- **Persistent kingdom growth:** resources → reclaim land → player-selected buildings → upgrades → permanent power/economy → more expansion.
- **Guild / Realm strategy:** persistent identity, rival powers, preparation phases, coordinated limited attacks, scoring, results, rewards and standings.
- **Hunter RPG layer:** progression, equipment, tactical combat, bosses and postgame hunts.
- **Post-apocalyptic reclamation:** scavenging, salvage, ruined territory and visible settlement rebuilding.
- **Gothic-horror presentation:** oppressive Enclave/Marches atmosphere and Hunter/boss identity.
- **Mobile-first usability:** core screens and actions must remain usable at phone viewport size.

## Temporary art exception

The current Vanguard, Duelist and Physician SVGs are stability placeholders. They deliberately replace the old Safari-corrupting WebP path and are **not** the final main-character sprite direction. Sprite replacement is an art-production task and must not alter the established gameplay/design pillars.

## Human playtest focus

Report anything that blocks progression; becomes unclear without explanation; fails to persist after reload; makes kingdom construction feel cosmetic rather than consequential; makes Guild/Realm wars feel disconnected from long-term growth; produces broken or unreadable mobile layouts; or makes any major screen feel like a web dashboard rather than the intended gothic strategy RPG.
